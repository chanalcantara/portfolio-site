About

I am a Registered Electronics Engineer (ECE) pursuing a Master of Science in Electronics Engineering at Mapúa University, currently working toward doctoral studies. My research centers on ubiquitous computing, context-aware embedded systems, pervasive IoT, and cyber-physical systems for safety-critical and human-centered applications — work that currently spans FreeRTOS-based hardware interfaces for nuclear reactor simulation and IoT-enabled smart laboratory environments.

My path to research has run through both industry and academia: as a Lead Electronics R&D Engineer deploying industrial IoT platforms in Fiji, as a Project Technical Specialist at the DOST–Advanced Science and Technology Institute developing national R&D hardware, and now as an Instructor I at Colegio de San Juan de Letran, where I integrate AI and IoT frameworks into engineering education while supervising capstone research. I hold the Associate ASEAN Engineer (AAE) recognition and am pursuing a PhD to deepen this research into ubiquitous and cyber-physical computing.

Research Interests

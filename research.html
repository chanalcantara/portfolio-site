Overview

My research centers on ubiquitous computing, context-aware embedded systems, pervasive IoT, and cyber-physical systems for safety-critical and human-centered applications. As a Registered Electronics Engineer (ECE) with a background spanning industrial IoT deployment, national R&D hardware development, and engineering education, I approach these problems from both the hardware and systems level — designing real-time embedded architectures that sense, reason about, and safely act within their physical environment. Current work spans FreeRTOS-based hardware interfaces for nuclear reactor simulation and IoT-enabled smart laboratory environments.

Areas of Interest

Ubiquitous & Context-Aware Computing

Designing embedded systems that sense and adapt to their physical and human context — from laboratory environments to industrial and agricultural settings — so that computation is embedded into, rather than separate from, the environments it serves.

Pervasive IoT & Cyber-Physical Systems

Architecting distributed sensing and control platforms — including Industrial IoT (IIoT) deployments for agricultural monitoring and smart building management — that bridge physical infrastructure with real-time data acquisition and autonomous response.

Embedded Real-Time Systems for Safety-Critical Applications

Developing FreeRTOS-based real-time firmware and finite-state-machine control architectures for safety-critical hardware interfaces, including nuclear reactor control simulation and laboratory safety and energy-optimization systems.

Current / Thesis Research

My M.S. thesis, "Design and Development of a TRIGA Research Reactor Simulator Based on a Legacy Control Console," integrates analog nuclear control hardware with real-time FreeRTOS firmware and UART-based simulator communication for operator training, at Mapúa University. In parallel, I lead development of an IoT-enabled smart laboratory system for safety and energy optimization in academic environments, and an AI-assisted sign language recognition application applying computer vision and embedded ML pipelines to clinical healthcare settings.
